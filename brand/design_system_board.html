Brand & design system · v1 · ernestholmes.us

A sun rising out of an open book. The book is the Science of Mind text; the sun is the realisation that comes from reading it. Holmes' own language is the brief: light, not fire. The mark is calm, frontal and symmetrical, and that restraint is the brand — this is a teacher, not a product launch.

Indigo is the ground and the mark's body. Gold is the sun and the one accent. There is no third colour. Everything else is paper, ink, and a decorative pastel wash that carries no meaning. This page is built from the system's own tokens — the colours, type and components below are the live values, not pictures of them. Switch the theme above and the whole page moves with it.

The mark

Two drawings, one mark

Which drawing you use is decided by the size it will be seen at, not by taste. Both are approved; neither supersedes the other.

Seven rays wherever seven rays can be seen; five rays where they cannot.
7-ray · ≥32px
5-ray · <32px, favicons
transparent · light grounds

Where 32px comes from

Both drawings were rendered down a ladder and compared. At 16px both collapse and five rays hold a hint of points where seven do not; at 20–24px five rays win clearly; at 32px both read; at 48px and up the seven-ray drawing is plainly finer.

Top row seven rays, bottom row five. Favicons take the small drawing at every frame size — one .ico ships one drawing, and splitting it would change the icon's shape as the OS picks a frame.

Rules

  • Never redraw, recolour, crop or rotate it. Use the files as they are.
  • The sun is always gold #F7B500. It never inverts. Only the book changes ink.
  • The book follows the ground. Indigo on light, white on dark. An indigo book on a dark ground disappears — the most likely mistake.
  • Clear space: the radius of the sun's disc on all four sides. Nothing enters it.
  • Minimum size: 24px for the transparent mark, 16px for a tile. Below that, no mark.
  • Never put the transparent mark on gold, on a photograph, or on the backdrop wash.
  • The wordmark is live type, not artwork — Satoshi Bold, composed at run time.

Colour

One accent, two golds

Gold is the brand's weak point and the token set is built around it. --accent is gold as text; --accent-bright is gold as fill. Swapping them is the standard way to break contrast here.

Three rules the compiler cannot enforce

  • Gold is never text on a light ground. Use --accent.
  • Gold is never a status. That is --danger and --success.
  • Colour is never the only signal. Focus is a ring and an offset.

Type

Satoshi, and nothing else

Self-hosted variable, weights 300–900, under the ITF Free Font License. No serif, no mono. The legacy serif wordmark is artwork, not a typeface the brand owns.
