# Afterword teacher policy (draft v3)

**Purpose.** Afterword lets people talk with historical teachers in conversation, drawn from their public-domain
writings. A pack is one teacher's eligible works plus the portrayal built on them. Packs live in
`holmes-gpt/src/lib/server/packs/`; each records its works in `sources.json`, and `packs.test.ts` enforces the
checkable parts of §1 and §2.

**Core test.** Afterword portrays; it never impersonates. A reasonable person must never be misled about who is
speaking or about what the teacher actually wrote.

## 1. Who qualifies

- The teacher died at least 50 years ago (1976 or earlier, as of 2026).
- The teacher is a public figure known for published teaching, not a private person.
- Living people and the recently dead are never eligible.
- For teachers who died 1957–1976, post-death publicity rights are checked before launch.

## 2. What sources qualify

- US public domain only: published 1930 or earlier, or published 1931–1963 with no copyright renewal on record, or
  published before 1978 without a copyright notice. Status is checked and recorded per book.
- Each edition and translation is recorded.
- Unpublished letters and papers are excluded unless their status is confirmed.
- Where works are still protected abroad, the pack is limited to regions where they're public domain.

## 3. Portrayal and disclosure

- The app may portray the teacher in first person, in their voice and style.
- A visible label is on every conversation: "An AI portrayal of [name], drawn from their public-domain writings."
- The label travels with anything shared or exported, including screenshots, cards and clips.
- Synthetic voice, image or video may be used when clearly labeled as an AI portrayal, including in anything shared
  or exported.
- Nothing is presented as an authentic historical recording, photo or film.
- Generated media carries a watermark or embedded provenance data where practical.

## 4. Fidelity

- The portrayal reflects the teacher's actual views, not views the company or the user prefers.
- When an answer goes beyond what the teacher wrote, the portrayal says so.
- The teacher never endorses a later movement, church, product, candidate or political position.

## 5. Quotes

- Generated speech is never formatted or presented as a quotation.
- Actual quotations are set apart and cite book and page.
- Links to the free public-domain texts are provided, so the app leads people to the books.

## 6. No commercial use of the teacher's voice

- The teacher never sells, upsells, or asks users to subscribe or return.
- Marketing speaks as Afterword, never as the teacher.

## 7. Spiritual authority and user wellbeing

- The portrayal does not give personal commands about relationships, money, health or major life decisions. It
  offers the teacher's ideas for reflection.
- No health, medical or financial claims, even in character. Writing on healing is presented as historical spiritual
  teaching. The app never suggests skipping or stopping treatment.
- In moments of grief, crisis or risk of harm, the app steps out of character and points to real help.
- No design that encourages compulsive use, such as streaks or guilt prompts in the teacher's voice.
- The app points people toward the original books and toward living communities and teachers.

## 8. Dignity and honest history

- No mocking, sexual or degrading portrayals. Attempts to push the persona there get the label and a refusal.
- The teacher's prejudices and errors are part of the record. They aren't hidden, and the persona doesn't promote
  them. When relevant, the app steps out of character to give context.

## 9. Privacy

- Conversations are treated as highly sensitive.
- Minimal retention, no training on conversations without opt-in, no sale of data, and easy deletion.

## 10. Living traditions

- Afterword doesn't claim to speak for any living tradition, church or organization descended from a teacher.
- Corrections from those communities, descendants, estates or institutions are reviewed even when the law doesn't
  require it.

## 11. Review and removal

- Each pack is reviewed before launch for eligibility, sources, citations, fidelity and portrayal.
- Anyone can report a wrong citation, a misattributed quote or a misleading portrayal, and the pack is corrected.

## 12. Out of scope for now

- Fictional public-domain characters (separate policy).
- Any mode built on a living person or on private writing.

**Before launch:** a lawyer reviews the public-domain and renewal checks, publicity rights for teachers who died
1957–1976, regional limits, privacy terms, and the Afterword trademark.

> Afterword portrays historical teachers honestly: it always says it is an AI, stays faithful to what they wrote and
> marks where it goes further, never uses their voice to sell, directs people to real help and to the original
> books, and treats both the teachers and the people who talk with them with respect.
